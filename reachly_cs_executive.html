How we attack churn

A 5-level churn-attack framework

  • 1
    Predict — a 50-parameter health score flags risk 90–120 days out.
  • 2
    Onboard — fix the pre-90 leak; get accounts to first value fast.
  • 3
    Engage — signal-triggered retention playbooks, run by humans or bots by tier.
  • 4
    Recover — automated dunning & collections on involuntary churn.
  • 5
    Expand — surface expansion signals to AEs (NRR upside).

How we de-risk the automation bet

Back-test → Pilot → Roll out

  • 1
    Back-test (Wks 1–4) — run AI logic on 6 months of history. Gate: churn-prediction precision ≥65%, payment-recovery ≥70%. No live customer action.
  • 2
    Pilot (Months 2–3) — supervised 2,000-account cohort, human override on. Gate: CSAT ≥4.0, deflection ≥50%, save-rate ≥ baseline.
  • 3
    Roll out (Month 4+) — full 20,000 accounts only after gates pass. Fallback: CRM-rules scoring if a gate fails.

The one big trade-off

ARR-first concentration

We deliberately hand the 16,000-account tail to automation, not a CSM. The bet: bots give the tail some proactive touch at near-zero cost — instead of zero.

✅ Why it's right

A human only pays back above ~$12k ACV. Forcing automation on the tail is what makes the 1.51% cost ratio possible.

👀 What we watch

Tail GRR, deflection and digital CSAT. If automation under-delivers, this is the assumption we revisit first.
