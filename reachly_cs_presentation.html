Account Movement — Scenarios & Guardrails

How accounts re-tier as ARR grows or contracts — and how we prevent thrashing

SCENARIO 1 Growth: SMB → Mid Market

  • Trailing 3-month average ARR crosses $12k threshold (+15% buffer = $13.8k)
  • Holds for 2 consecutive months at confirmed level
  • CSM assigned within 2 weeks of confirmation
  • Warm handoff: digital specialist → named CSM with full account notes
  • Treated as buying signal — CSM scoped for expansion opportunity with AE

SCENARIO 2 Contraction: MM → SMB

  • Trailing average drops below $12k (buffer: must be below $10.2k)
  • CSM does NOT leave immediately — 2-quarter (6-month) hold before demotion
  • 6-month window is a save play: CSM actively works to recover seats
  • If account recovers: stays with CSM, no disruption to relationship
  • If still below at month 6: warm handoff to pooled SMB with full context

SCENARIO 3 Signal-Based Pre-Promotion

  • Account below $12k but shows leading signals: 3+ consecutive months of seat adds, new team activating, inbound to AE
  • CSM assigned proactively before threshold is crossed
  • CS surfaces expansion opportunity to AE — this is the NRR engine
  • When account crosses $12k: CSM already owns the relationship (no disruption)
  • Pre-promotion tracked via ChurnZero usage velocity score

SCENARIO 4 Payment Failure Path

  • Day 1: Stripe Smart Retries (automated, 3 attempts)
  • Day 3: Churnkey retry logic + save-offer email sequence
  • Day 7: Human call for accounts >$500 MRR — not automated
  • Day 14: Unresolved → "Delinquent at-risk" flag in ChurnZero
  • Day 30: Still unresolved → treated as churned. Target recovery: 33%

The 5 Non-Negotiable Guardrails

1
Trailing 3-month average only. No spot-rate re-tiering. A one-month seat spike or dip changes nothing.
2
±15% hysteresis band. Account must clear the threshold by 15% before review is triggered. Prevents boundary thrashing.
3
Promote in 2 weeks. Demote in 2 quarters. Asymmetric: growth is a buying signal, contraction is a save opportunity.
4
No cold handoffs. Every transition has a warm intro and 30-day overlap. Outgoing owner holds until new owner confirms pickup in HubSpot.
5
Account is always owned. No account exists without a tier assignment, a motion, and a coverage owner — even if that owner is an automated sequence.
